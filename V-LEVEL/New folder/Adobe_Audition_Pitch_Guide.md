# Adobe Audition – Change Voice Pitch Naturally

1. Open your MP3 file in **Adobe Audition**.
2. Go to **Effects → Time and Pitch → Stretch and Pitch**.
3. Set **Algorithm** to **iZotope Radius**.
4. Open **Advanced Settings**.
5. Enable:
   - **Solo Instrument or Voice**
   - **Preserve Speech Characteristics**
6. Keep **Formant Shift = 0**.
7. Adjust **Pitch Shift**:
   - `-1` semitone → slightly lower voice
   - `-2` semitones → lower voice
   - `+1` semitone → slightly higher voice
   - `+2` semitones → higher voice
8. Preview the result.
9. If it sounds natural, click **Apply**.
10. Export with **File → Export → File** and choose **MP3** or **WAV**.

## Recommended Settings

```text
Algorithm: iZotope Radius
Solo Instrument or Voice: ON
Preserve Speech Characteristics: ON
Formant Shift: 0
Pitch Shift: -1 to -2 semitones
```

For natural speech, avoid very large pitch changes because artifacts can become more noticeable.
